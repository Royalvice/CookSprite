# Offline HDRI provenance

These low-resolution HDRIs are redistributed from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)
under its CC0 license. They are bundled so the normal-map lighting preview is
fully functional offline. The environment affects lighting by default; users
may optionally reveal it as the visible background.

| CookSprite id | Poly Haven asset | Upstream 1K HDR |
|---|---|---|
| `neutral_studio` | Poly Haven Studio | `poly_haven_studio_1k.hdr` |
| `bright_day` | Qwantani Dawn (Pure Sky) | `qwantani_dawn_puresky_1k.hdr` |
| `moon_night` | Rogland Moonlit Night | `rogland_moonlit_night_1k.hdr` |

Downloaded from `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/` on
2026-08-13. No network request is made by the application at runtime.
